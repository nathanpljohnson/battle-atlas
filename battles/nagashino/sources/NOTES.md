# Nagashino / Shitaragahara 1575: source notes

Date: Tenshō 3, 5th month, 21st day = 29 June 1575 (Julian, the calendar then in use in Europe) = 9 July 1575 (proleptic Gregorian). Field: Shitaragahara (設楽原) / Arumihara (あるみ原), west of Nagashino Castle, Mikawa (Shinshiro, Aichi).
