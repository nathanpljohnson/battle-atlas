# Franklin sources (fetched 2026-10-16)
- cox1897.txt: J. D. Cox, *The Battle of Franklin, Tennessee* (Scribner, 1897), archive.org battleoffranklin00coxj. Commander of the Union line; quotes O.R. vol. 45 pt 1 at length. Strengths p.209-211, Confederate losses p.211-213, Union losses table p.215, sunset 4:59 local (Porter, Cincinnati Obs.) p.147n.
- hood1880.txt: J. B. Hood, *Advance and Retreat* (1880), pp. 292-295. Confederate commander's account (self-justifying): 4 p.m. advance, artillery withheld for civilians, Johnson's division before dark, fighting to 9 p.m., withdrawal ~3 a.m.; claims ~1,000 prisoners; losses 4,500 (O.R. 45/1 p.654).
- wp_franklin.wiki / wp_battle_section.txt: Wikipedia, built on Jacobson & Rupp (2006), Sword (1992), McDonough & Connelly (1983), Eicher (2001).
- abt.txt: American Battlefield Trust battle page: 4:00 step-off, ~20,000 attackers, losses 2,326 / 6,252.
- tn_encyclopedia.txt: P. Craddock, Tennessee Encyclopedia (Tenn. Hist. Soc.): 4:30 step-off, ~23,000 vs ~15,000 in the line, fight to ~10 p.m.
- Maps: jesp_1600.png / jesp_1630.png (Hal Jespersen, CC BY 3.0, brigade positions 4-4:30 and 4:30-dark, after Jacobson); fieldworks.jpg (LOC 2003627083, 1864 "Field works at Franklin", south-up); twining_battlefield.jpg (LOC 2003630453, Twining's 1864 engineer sketch, rotated); bl_map.jpg (Battles & Leaders vol. 4).
